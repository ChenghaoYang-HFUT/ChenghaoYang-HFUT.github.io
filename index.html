About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical Engineering at Hefei University of Technology. My research focuses on the interaction between unsteady flow and sound, especially aerodynamic noise generation and its control through flow manipulation.

My doctoral work investigates forced rotation of circular cylinders, combining computational fluid dynamics with aeroacoustic analysis to understand how changes in the wake and near-field pressure affect sound radiation.
