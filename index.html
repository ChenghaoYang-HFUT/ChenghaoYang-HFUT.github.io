About

My research focuses on flow and aerodynamic noise control. I study how flow manipulation changes unsteady flow structures and sound generation, with applications to airfoils, circular cylinders and rod–airfoil configurations.

My work combines computational fluid dynamics and aeroacoustic analysis to investigate active and passive control methods, including blowing, forced rotation, trailing-edge serrations and geometric modifications. I am also interested in artificial intelligence for flow analysis and control.

Research

Flow control

Active and passive manipulation of flow separation, boundary layers and wakes around airfoils and bluff bodies.

Aerodynamic noise control

Understanding and reducing self-noise and interaction noise through blowing, forced rotation and geometric modifications.

Computational methods

Computational fluid dynamics and aeroacoustic analysis, with an interest in AI-assisted flow analysis and control.
